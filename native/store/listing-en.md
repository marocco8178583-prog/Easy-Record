# Store texts — English
**Name:** Easy Record
**Subtitle (≤30):** Work log, hours & payments
**Short description (≤80):** Log work days and hours, track payments and debts, reports and backup.
**Full description:**
Easy Record helps freelancers and tradespeople see at any moment how much they worked, how much they earned and who still owes them.
- Time clock with breaks and overtime
- Clients, projects and per-client rates
- Partial payments, open debts and money you owe others
- Expenses and profit per client and month
- Receipts and voice notes for each work day
- Tasks and reminders, voice commands and spoken summaries
- PDF, Excel and CSV reports
- Works offline, syncs across devices, full backup
Easy Record does not issue legal invoices or receipts and is not tax advice.
